Logo

Four versions of the InkMoon Games logo. Pick the one that fits the background and the space.

Choosing a Version

  • Color

    The main version. Use it on light backgrounds whenever full color is available.

    • color.svg

  • Black and White

    For print, stamps, documents and any place limited to one color.

    • Blackandwhite.svg

  • Reverse

    For dark backgrounds, photos and the dark mode of a page. Pick it whenever the dark logo would blend in.

    • reverse.svg

  • Favicon

    For browser tabs, app icons and any space too small for the full logo.

    • Favicon.svg

Sizing and Clear Space

  • Large (400px or wider): website headers, title screens, posters and banners, where the full logo with its text can be read.
  • Medium (120 to 400px): store pages, social media posts and menus. Keep the "InkMoon Games" text readable.
  • Small (under 120px): use the favicon instead of the full logo, since the text becomes too small to read.
  • Always scale the logo proportionally and leave empty space around it, at least the height of the "I" in "Ink".

File Formats

  • Use a vector format first (SVG). It stays sharp at every size, from a tab icon to a billboard, and has a small file size.
  • Use PNG only where SVG is not supported.
  • Avoid JPG for the logo. It has no transparent background and adds blur around the edges.

Do's and Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Use the Color version on light backgrounds.
  • Use the Reverse version on dark backgrounds, photos and dark mode.
  • Scale proportionally and leave empty space of at least the height of the "I" in "Ink".
  • Use the favicon under 120px. Use SVG first, PNG only where SVG isn't supported.

✕ Don't

  • Don't change the colors of the logo.
  • Don't add effects such as shadows, glows, outlines or gradients.
